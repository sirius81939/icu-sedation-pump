Label & Safety
The bolus is calculated from undiluted stock (5,000 U/mL), NOT from the 25 U/mL infusion bag. Methylprednisolone · Spinal Cord Injury

Stock: 500 mg/vial. Historical NASCIS high-dose regimen for acute traumatic spinal cord injury; NOT routine standard care. Specialist discussion and shared risk assessment required.

Infusion protocol
IV BOLUS · 15 MIN—
IV DRIP DOSE—
IV DRIP DURATION—
Suitable situations · Historical, optional regimen in carefully selected adults with acute traumatic spinal cord injury, started within 8 hours, only after spine/neurosurgery consultation. Not routine therapy.
Use caution / less suitable · Major infection, GI bleeding, uncontrolled hyperglycemia, immunosuppression or polytrauma. Increased pneumonia, sepsis, GI bleeding and other complications, especially with 48-hour treatment.
Not for · Isolated traumatic brain injury, nonspecific elevated intracranial pressure, or initiation beyond 8 hours after spinal cord injury. Steroids do not replace urgent decompression, stabilization and rehabilitation consultation.
Label & Safety
Controversial historical NASCIS protocol: 30 mg/kg IV over 15 minutes, wait 45 minutes after the bolus ends, then 5.4 mg/kg/hr for 23 hours (24-hour protocol) or 47 hours (48-hour protocol). Evidence for neurological benefit is uncertain; many modern guidelines recommend against routine methylprednisolone in acute spinal cord injury. A 2017 AO Spine guideline weakly suggests offering 24-hour MPSS within 8 hours as an option, recommends against offering after 8 hours and against 48-hour infusion. Do not interpret the historical 3–8 hour subgroup findings as established superiority of 48 hours. Vial is 500 mg; dilution and reconstitution remain unverified, so mL/hr is not displayed. Seek spine/neurosurgery and rehabilitation input.
